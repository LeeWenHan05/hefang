Crisp Pear 沁梨

Crisp · Juicy · Gentle

Taste
Crisp · Juicy · Gentle
Key ingredients
Pear · Apple · Jujube · Goji
Tea leaves
None in the recipe
WHY THESE INGREDIENTS

Fresh, with a soft finish.

Pear tastes fresh and light. Apple makes the drink rounder and a little sweeter. Jujube adds warmth, while goji gives a soft fruit-and-herb finish.

HOW IT TASTES

Fresh first, warm at the end.

It starts crisp and juicy. The sweetness is light, and the finish is soft with a little dried-fruit warmth.

WHERE THE IDEA COMES FROM

A familiar homemade drink, made brighter.

Pear, jujube and goji are often used in homemade drinks. We keep that familiar feeling, then make it brighter with apple and fresh pear.

Taste
Tangy · Roasted · Deep

WHY THESE INGREDIENTS

Tangy, with a roasted finish.

Smoked plum and hawthorn bring the deep, tangy taste. Mandarin peel adds a fresh citrus aroma and light bitterness. Oolong gives the drink a warm roasted finish.

HOW IT TASTES

Bold, but not heavy.

It starts tangy, then moves into citrus and roasted tea. The finish is dry and slightly smoky, with less sweetness than the other blends.

WHERE THE IDEA COMES FROM

Inspired by traditional smoked-plum drinks.

We keep the familiar depth of smoked-plum drinks, then make it lighter and more balanced with hawthorn, mandarin peel and oolong.

THE HEFANG WAY

From fresh fruit
to your bottle.

All five drinks follow the same careful process. The cooking time changes with each recipe, but the goal stays the same: keep the fruit fresh-tasting and let every ingredient be easy to notice.

  1. 01
    FRESH START

    Select and wash

    We check the fresh fruit, wash it well and prepare it for cooking.

  2. 02
    MEASURE & PREPARE

    Prepare the ingredients

    The fruit is cut, the dried ingredients are rinsed, and everything is measured for each recipe.

  3. 03
    GENTLE HEAT

    Gently simmer

    We simmer the ingredients slowly instead of boiling them hard, so the flavour stays light and balanced.

  4. 04
    COOL & BOTTLE

    Strain, cool & bottle

    The drink is strained, cooled and bottled, ready to enjoy.

We describe taste, ingredients and traditional inspiration. HEFANG drinks are not medicines and do not promise health results.
